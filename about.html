About Janelle

I'm current a UI/UX Designer at Capital One. I am part of their Design Development Program (DDP) where I am in my first rotation team with Capital One Auto.

I graduated with a Bachelors of Science in Human Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University within the School of Computer Science. I have a minor in game design.

Outside of work, I drum and manage my indie rock band Ejector Crab and I am a mentor at Curious Cardinals.

In university, I worked as a TA and Grader for multiple HCI classes, usually catered towards Masters students. I spent my summers teaching for Girls Who Code and conducting independent research through Carnegie Mellon.
